◆ OUR APPROACH TO SOURCES ◆

WaqtX treats Islamic knowledge with honesty and care. Every item in our history database is tagged with its source type and certainty level — so you always know the basis of what you are reading.

Quran Direct verse references. The Quran is the primary source and takes precedence in all religious matters.
Hadith Referenced with collection, chapter, and hadith number. Grading (sahih/hasan/etc.) noted where relevant.
Classical Source Traditional Muslim scholarship — Sirah, Tabari, Ibn Sa'd, al-Baladhuri, and others. These are our primary historical narratives.
Academic Modern historical scholarship. Valuable for context and corroboration, but distinct from religious authority.
Disputed Where different historical accounts exist, we note the disagreement rather than presenting one version as unquestionable fact.

This approach makes WaqtX more credible, not less. Honest acknowledgment of what is established vs. debated is a sign of scholarly integrity — a value central to the Islamic tradition of ‘ilm (knowledge).
